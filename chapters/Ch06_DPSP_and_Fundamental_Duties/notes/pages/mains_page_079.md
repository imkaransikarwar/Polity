## Chapter 7: Fundamental Duties

FUNDAMENTAL DUTIES The 42nd Amendment added Fundamental Duties to the Indian Constitution (Swaran Singh Committee recommendation) in 1976 through Part IV-A and Article 51-A, initially listing ten duties. Fundamental Duties emphasize the moral obligations and commitments of citizens towards society and the nation, highlighting the inseparable nature of rights and duties.

### Fundamental Duties (Article 51A)

- **1.** To abide by the Constitution and respect its ideals, institutions, the National Flag, and National Anthem.

- **2.** To cherish and follow the noble ideals of the national struggle for freedom.

- **3.** To uphold and protect the sovereignty, unity, and integrity of India.

- **4.** To defend the country and render national service when called upon.

- **5.** Promote harmony and brotherhood and renounce practices derogatory to dignity of women

- **6.** To value and preserve the composite culture of our heritage.

- **7.** To protect and improve the natural environment (forests, lakes, rivers, wildlife).

- **8.** To develop scientific temper, humanism, and the spirit of inquiry.

- **9.** To safeguard public property and abjure violence.

- **10.** To strive towards excellence in all spheres of individual and collective activity.

- **11.** To provide educational opportunities to children/wards aged 6–14 years (Added by 86th Amendment, 2002).

### Laws Implementing Fundamental Duties

- **1.** Prevention of Insults to National Honour Act (1971): Prevents disrespect to the Flag, Constitution, and Anthem.

- **2.** Protection of Civil Rights Act (1955): Implements the duty to promote brotherhood and equality.

- **3.** Wildlife Protection Act (1972) & Forest Conservation Act (1980): Enforce the duty to protect the natural environment.

- **4.** Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act (1967): Related to the duty to uphold sovereignty and integrity.

- **5.** Right to Education Act (2009): Gives statutory effect to the 11th duty regarding child education.

### Significance of Fundamental Duties

- **1.** Constitutional Reminder: Acts as a constant reminder that citizens must perform duties while enjoying their rights.

- **2.** Warning Against Anti-Socials: Serves as a warning against activities like burning the Flag or destroying public property.

- **3.** Judicial Interpretation Tool: Courts use duties to determine the "reasonableness" of restrictions on fundamental rights.

- **4.** Promoting Patriotism: Inculcates a sense of discipline and commitment toward the nation's progress and unity.
