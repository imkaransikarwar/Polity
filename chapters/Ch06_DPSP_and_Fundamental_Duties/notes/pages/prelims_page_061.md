#### Q18 (2011)

**Under the Constitution of India, which one of the following is not a Fundamental Duty?**

- **(a)** To vote in public elections
- **(b)** To develop the scientific temper
- **(c)** To safeguard public property
- **(d)** To abide by the Constitution and respect its ideals

> **Answer:** `(a)` · [Prelims Source Page 61](../../pages/prelims/prelims_page_061.pdf)

#### Q20 (2008)

**Which of the following is/are included in the Directive Principles of State Policy?**

- **1.** Prohibition of traffic in human beings and forced labour
- **2.** Prohibition of consumption except for medicinal purposes of intoxicating drinks and of other drugs which are injurious to health

*Select the correct answer using the code given below:*

- **(a)** 1 only
- **(b)** 2 only
- **(c)** Both 1 and 2
- **(d)** Neither 1 nor 2

> **Answer:** `(b)` · [Prelims Source Page 61](../../pages/prelims/prelims_page_061.pdf)

| PARAMETER | FUNDAMENTAL RIGHTS | DIRECTIVE PRINCIPLES OF STATE POLICY | FUNDAMENTAL DUTIES |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Part | Part III | Part IV | Part IV-A |
| Articles | Article 12 to 35 | Article 36 to 51 | Article 51A |
| Source of inspiration | Constitution of the United States. | Constitution of Ireland, which had itself borrowed from the Spanish Constitution. | Constitution of the erstwhile Soviet Union (USSR). |
| Concept | Supreme human rights recognised to keep the individual away from the oppression of a transient majority. | Social and economic policies to be pursued by the Government. | A constant reminder to citizens for observing certain basic norms while they enjoy Fundamental Rights. |
| Example | Article 21: Right to life is a Fundamental Right. | Article 42: Just and humane conditions of work and maternity relief. | Article 51A(a): To abide by the Constitution and respect its ideals and institutions, the National Flag and the National Anthem. |
| Type of democracy secured [PRELIMS 2015] | Political democracy. | Economic and social democracy; the Directive Principles spell out socio-economic democracy in the country. | Not a democracy-securing device; a civic obligation supporting both. |
| Nature | Negative, as they prohibit the State from doing certain things. | Positive, as they require the State to do certain things. | Mixed: partly positive obligations (to develop the scientific temper, to strive towards excellence) and partly prohibitions (to abjure violence, to renounce practices derogatory to the dignity of women). |
| Whose welfare | Promote the welfare of the individual; hence | Promote the welfare of the community; hence societarian and socialistic. | Regulate the conduct of the individual citizen in the interest of the community. |
