Education & Child Care (Article 45)

- **1.** Right to Education (RTE): Article 45 was upgraded to a Fundamental Right (Article 21A) in 2002.

- **2.** Early Childhood Care: The PM POSHAN (formerly Mid-Day Meal) and Anganwadi services now cater to over 10 crore children and pregnant women. Environment & Wildlife (Article 48A)

- **1.** Forest Cover: India is one of the few countries where forest cover has stabilized/increased in the 21st century (approx. 24.6% of geographical area).

- **2.** Project Tiger & Lion: Success in wildlife conservation has seen tiger populations cross 3,900 by 2025-26.
