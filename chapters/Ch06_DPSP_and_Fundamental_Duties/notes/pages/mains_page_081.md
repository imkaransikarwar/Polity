Arguments Against (Anti-Enforceability)

- **1.** State Tyranny and Surveillance: Enforceability could be misused by the government to suppress dissent or label any criticism of the state as a violation of "Duty toward the nation."

- **2.** Vagueness of Language: Terms like "scientific temper" or "noble ideals" are too subjective. Legal enforceability can lead to "Judicial Overreach"..

- **3.** Burden on the Judiciary: As of 2026, Indian courts already face a backlog of 50 million cases. Enforceable duties would trigger a massive wave of "frivolous" litigation between citizens.

- **4.** Violation of Liberty: Forced "excellence" or "patriotism" contradicts the essence of a liberal democracy.

- **5.** Redundancy: Most critical duties (protecting sovereignty, environment, public property) are already covered by specific laws like the IPC/BNS, Forest Act, and UAPA.
