| Adoption of the Constitution, 26 Nov 1949 | **1.** The Assembly 'adopted, enacted and gave to itself' the Constitution - Preamble + 395 Articles + 22 Parts + 8 Schedules.<br>**2.** It has been celebrated as Constitution Day / Samvidhan Divas since 2015 (earlier observed as National Law Day). | On this date the Constitution was adopted, not commenced. But 15 Articles came into force at once under Art 394, so that elections could be held and institutions set up before 26 Jan 1950:<br>**(a)** Citizenship: Arts 5 to<br>**9.**<br>**(b)** Oath of the President: Art 60.<br>**(c)** Elections: Art 324.<br>**(d)** Interpretation: Arts 366, 367.<br>**(e)** Transitional provisions: Arts 379, 380, 388, 391, 392, 393. |
| Manuscript, calligraphy & artwork, 1949 to 1950 | **1.** The Constitution was handwritten, not printed - flowing italic calligraphy by Prem Behari Narain Raizada, who charged no fee; 2. decorated by artists of Santiniketan under Acharya Nandalal Bose, with the Preamble page illuminated by Beohar Rammanohar Sinha;<br>**3.** 22 miniature-style illustrations.<br>**4.** Originals preserved in helium-filled cases in the Parliament Library. | **1.** Two handwritten originals - Hindi and English - both signed on 24 Jan 1950.<br>**2.** Raizada's only condition was that his name appear on every page and his grandfather's on the last page.<br>**3.** The 22 illustrations correspond to the 22 Parts, tracing Indian civilisation from Mohenjo-daro to the freedom movement. |
| Signing & final session, 24 Jan 1950 | **1.** Last session of the Constituent Assembly.<br>**2.** 284 members signed the Constitution (the Hindi and English handwritten copies) in Constitution Hall; 3. 'Jana Gana Mana' adopted as the National Anthem and 'Vande Mataram' given equal status as the National Song;<br>**4.** Dr Rajendra Prasad was elected the first President of India. | **1.** Three dates routinely swapped: adopted 26 Nov 1949 · signed 24 Jan 1950 · commenced 26 Jan 1950.<br>**2.** The Anthem and Song were adopted on 24 Jan 1950, not on 26 Nov 1949 and not on Republic Day.<br>**3.** It rained that day, which Prasad noted as an omen. |
| Commencement, 26 Jan 1950 | **1.** The Constitution came into force;<br>**2.** India became a sovereign democratic republic.<br>**3.** Art 395 repealed the Indian Independence Act 1947 and the Government of India Act 1935 (with all amendments and supplementary Acts).<br>**4.** The date was chosen to honour the Purna Swaraj declaration of 26 Jan 1930, resolved at the Lahore session of Dec 1929. | **1.** Art 395 repealed the 1935 and 1947 Acts but expressly NOT the Abolition of Privy Council Jurisdiction Act, 1949.<br>**2.** 26 Jan = Republic Day (commencement);<br>**3.** 26 Nov = Constitution Day (adoption); |
