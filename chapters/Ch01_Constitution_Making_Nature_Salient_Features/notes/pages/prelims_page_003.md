## CHAPTER 01: MAKING OF THE INDIAN CONSTITUTION

### THEME ANALYSIS

#### 1. REPEATS

#### A. Legacy of the Colonial Acts

- Government of India Act, 1935: residuary powers with the Governor General [2018].

- It’s Centre State scheme borrowed into our Constitution [2012].

- Instrument of Instructions becoming the Directive Principles of State Policy [2010].

- The All India Federation that never came into being [2009, 2024].

- Simon Commission seeking responsible government in place of dyarchy [2010].

#### B. Missions and Plans

- Cripps Mission: Dominion Status, not full independence [2016, 2009].

- Its Constitution making body [2009] and provincial opt out clause [2022].

- Sent by Churchill [2009]; Nehru and Azad negotiated [2010]; rejection led to Quit India [2013].

- Simla Conference: Indians in every portfolio, including War Member [2008].

- Cabinet Mission recommended a federal government only [2015].

#### C. The Constituent Assembly

- Members indirectly elected by Provincial Legislative Assemblies [2013].

- Dr Sachchidananda Sinha as Provisional President before Dr Rajendra Prasad [2024].

- Drafting Committee under Ambedkar, set up 29 August 1947 [2023].

#### D. The Format Itself

- Half true statements on federation and composition [2009, 2015, 2022, 2024].

- Statement I and Statement II pairing of date with explanation [2023].

- Absolutes such as full independence mark wrong options.

#### 2. HOW THE FORMAT HAS CHANGED

- 2008 to 2016: one line recall, mostly single answer stems on missions and the 1935 Act [2008, 2009, 2010, 2013, 2016]

- 2022 and 2024: two statement sets on precise clauses, the Cripps opt out [2022] and the unrealised 1935 federation [2024]

- 2023: the Statement I and Statement II format arrives, pairing a date with its explanation [2023]

#### 3. WHAT IS BEING ASKED NOW

- Clauses that failed or never operated: the 1935 federation, and Defence and Foreign Affairs as reserved subjects [2024]

- Exact dates and offices: the Drafting Committee date [2023], the Provisional President [2024]

- Expect one true and one false statement on a single Act or mission
