| Idea and demand for a Constituent Assembly, 1934 to 1942 | **1.** Idea first put forward by M.N. Roy (1934), a pioneer of the communist movement in India; 2. officially demanded by the Indian National Congress in 1935;<br>**3.** Nehru (1938) declared it must be elected on adult franchise; 4. conceded 'in principle' in the August Offer (1940);<br>**5.** The Cripps Mission (1942) offered a constitution-making body after the war - rejected by the Congress and the League. | **1.** The author of the demand is M.N. Roy, not Nehru or Gandhi.<br>**2.** The Assembly finally came under the Cabinet Mission Plan 1946, not the Cripps proposals - and, contrary to Nehru's 1938 demand, it was never elected on adult franchise. |
| Cripps Mission, Mar to Apr 1942 [PRELIMS 2022,2016,2013,2010, 2009] | **1.** Context: Sent by Winston Churchill’s War Cabinet as Japan advanced to India’s eastern borders after the fall of Rangoon (March 1942).<br>**2.** Leader: Sir Stafford Cripps (Lord Privy Seal and Leader of the House of Commons) arrived in Delhi in late March 1942; the Draft Declaration was published at the end of March 1942.<br>**3.** Main offer: Dominion status after the war for a new Indian Union, equal to other Dominions, with the right to secede from the Commonwealth; not immediate independence.<br>**4.** Constitution-making body (after the war):<br>&bull; Elected by the Lower Houses of Provincial Legislatures as a single electoral college, through proportional representation.<br>&bull; Princely States to nominate members in proportion to their population.<br>**5.** Right of non-accession: Any province unwilling to accept the new constitution could stay out and sign a separate agreement with Britain for equal status; seen as the seed of Pakistan.<br>**6.** During the war: Britain to retain control of defence.<br>**7.** Rejection:<br>&bull; Congress (negotiators Pt. Nehru and Maulana Azad, then Congress President) rejected it over defence and the lack of a real National Government; Gandhi called it a “post-dated cheque”.<br>&bull; Muslim League (no clear acceptance of Pakistan), Depressed Classes and Sikhs also rejected it. | **1.** [Prelims 2022] Constituent Assembly members were to be elected by the provincial lower houses - only the princely states' representatives were nominated; so 'nominated by the Provincial Assemblies' is FALSE, while the province's right to stay out and sign a separate agreement with Britain is TRUE.<br>**2.** [Prelims 2009 / 2016] Cripps promised a constitution-making body and Dominion status, never full independence.<br>**3.** [Prelims 2010] The Congress's official negotiators were Nehru and Azad, not Gandhi-Patel, not Kripalani-Rajagopalachari.<br>**4.** [Prelims 2009] The Mission was sent by Winston Churchill - not MacDonald (1931 RTC era), Baldwin or Chamberlain.<br>**5.** [Prelims 2013] The failure of the Cripps Proposals triggered the Quit India Movement (Bombay, 8 August 1942) - not the Cabinet Mission, Simon Report or Wavell Plan.<br>**6.** Note also that the Constituent Assembly that actually met came under the Cabinet Mission Plan 1946, not Cripps. |
