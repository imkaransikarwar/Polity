| Constituent Assembly as sovereign body & legislature, 15 Aug 1947 | **1.** From 15 Aug 1947 the Assembly became a fully sovereign body free to frame any constitution and to repeal or alter any Act of the British Parliament, including the Indian Independence Act itself.<br>**2.** It also assumed the role of the Dominion Legislature - 'Constituent Assembly (Legislative)' - first meeting 17 Nov 1947, Speaker G.V. Mavalankar. | **1.** Two hats, two chairs: as constitution-maker Dr Rajendra Prasad presided; 2. as legislature G.V. Mavalankar presided.<br>**3.** Same members, separate sittings - so the criticism 'not a sovereign body' is only true of 1946-47. |
| Drafting Committee, 29 Aug 1947 [PRELIMS 2023] | **1.** Set up by resolution on 29 August 1947 to scrutinise B.N. Rau's draft.<br>**2.** Seven members:<br>**(a)** Dr B.R. Ambedkar (Chairman), Alladi Krishnaswami Ayyar, N. Gopalaswami Ayyangar, K.M. Munshi, Syed Mohammad Saadulla.<br>**(b)** B.L. Mitter: resigned on health grounds, replaced by N. Madhava Rau.<br>**(c)** D.P. Khaitan: died in 1948, replaced by T.T. Krishnamachari. | [Prelims 2023] The Drafting Committee was set up on 29 Aug 1947, not on 26 Nov 1949, which is Constitution Day. The resolution named no chairman; the Committee elected Ambedkar at its first meeting on 30 Aug 1947. Membership was always seven, though the individuals changed. |
| Major committees - Nehru's three, 1947 | **1.** Union Powers Committee - Jawaharlal Nehru;<br>**2.** Union Constitution Committee - Jawaharlal Nehru;<br>**3.** States Committee (Committee for Negotiating with the States) - Jawaharlal Nehru. | **1.** Nehru chaired three of the eight major committees - MCQs habitually shift the Union Constitution Committee to Patel or Ambedkar.<br>**2.** The Union Powers Committee recommended a strong Centre, reversing the Objectives Resolution's promise of residuary powers to the units. |
| Major committees - Patel's two, 1947 | **1.** Provincial Constitution Committee - Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel;<br>**2.** Advisory Committee on Fundamental Rights, Minorities and Tribal & Excluded Areas - Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel. | **1.** Patel's Advisory Committee worked through sub-committees: Fundamental Rights - J.B. Kripalani;<br>**2.** Minorities - H.C. Mukherjee;<br>**3.** North-East Frontier Tribal Areas & Assam Excluded Areas - Gopinath Bardoloi;<br>**4.** Excluded & Partially Excluded Areas other than Assam - A.V. Thakkar.<br>**5.** Kripalani, not Ambedkar, chaired the Fundamental Rights sub-committee. |
| Major committees - Rajendra Prasad's, 1946 to 1949 | **1.** Committee on Rules of Procedure - Dr Rajendra Prasad;<br>**2.** Steering Committee - Dr Rajendra Prasad.<br>**3.** He also chaired the Finance and Staff Committee and the Ad hoc Committee on the National Flag. | **1.** Prasad chaired the procedural and housekeeping committees;<br>**2.** Ambedkar chaired only the Drafting Committee and was not a member of the Union Constitution or Union Powers Committees.<br>**3.** The 'eight major committees' = Union Powers, Union Constitution, Provincial Constitution, Drafting, Advisory, Rules of Procedure, States, Steering. |
