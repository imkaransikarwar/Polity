| First sitting & Dr Sachchidananda Sinha, 9 Dec 1946 [PRELIMS 2024] | **1.** First meeting in the Constitution Hall (now the Central Hall of Parliament House), New Delhi, attended by around 205-210 members including 9 women.<br>**2.** Dr Sachchidananda Sinha, the oldest member, was made temporary / provisional President following French practice.<br>**3.** The Muslim League boycotted and pressed for a separate Pakistan. | [Prelims 2024] The provisional President was Dr Sachchidananda Sinha - not Rajendra Prasad, Ambedkar, Rajagopalachari or T.T. Sir B.N. Rau appointed Constitutional Adviser to the Assembly - he prepared the original draft (Oct 1947) that the Drafting Committee scrutinised. | **1.** Rajendra Prasad presided over the Assembly and later became the first President of India (elected 24 Jan 1950, sworn in 26 Jan 1950).<br>**2.** B.N. Rau was an adviser, not a member of the Assembly's Drafting Committee - yet the first draft was his, not Ambedkar's. |
| Objectives Resolution, Moved 13 Dec 1946 · Adopted 22 Jan 1947 | **1.** Moved by Jawaharlal Nehru.<br>**2.** Declared India an Independent Sovereign Republic; 3. a Union of the British Indian territories, the princely states and other willing areas, with residuary powers in the autonomous units; 4. all power and authority derived from the people; 5. guaranteed justice - social, economic and political, equality of status and opportunity before the law, and freedom of thought, expression, belief, faith, worship, vocation, association and action subject to law and public morality; 6. safeguards for minorities, backward and tribal areas and depressed/other backward classes; 7. integrity of the territory and sovereign rights on land, sea and air; and India's contribution to world peace and human welfare. | **1.** It is the basis of the Preamble but NOT a verbatim copy.<br>**2.** It said 'Republic' in Dec 1946, when India was NOT even a Dominion.<br>**3.** It contains no mention of 'Socialist' or 'Secular' - those came with the 42nd Amendment, 1976.<br>**4.** It also promised the units residuary powers - which the final Constitution reversed (residuary powers to the Union, Art 248). |
| Indian Independence Act, 1947 | **1.** Based on the Mountbatten Plan (3 June 1947);<br>**2.** Royal assent 18 July 1947.<br>**3.** From 15 August 1947 two independent Dominions - India and Pakistan - each free to secede from the Commonwealth.<br>**4.** Crown paramountcy over ~560 princely states lapsed, leaving them free to join either Dominion or remain independent.<br>**5.** Ended British rule; abolished the office of Secretary of State for India; 6. dropped 'Emperor of India' from the royal title. | **1.** The Act gave Dominion status, NOT a republic - India became a republic only on 26 Jan 1950.<br>**2.** Governors-General became constitutional heads bound by ministers' advice.<br>**3.** Each Dominion got its own Governor-General - Mountbatten (India), Jinnah (Pakistan) - there was no common GG.<br>**4.** C. Rajagopalachari (1948) was the first Indian and last GG. |
| National Flag, 22 July 1947 | **1.** The Constituent Assembly adopted the National Flag on 22 July 1947, on a motion by Nehru.<br>**2.** The Ad hoc Committee on the National Flag was chaired by Dr Rajendra Prasad. | **1.** Keep the symbol dates apart: Flag 22 July 1947 · Anthem and Song 24 Jan 1950 · State Emblem (Sarnath Lion Capital) 26 Jan 1950.<br>**2.** The Ashoka Chakra replaced the charkha of the Congress flag. |
