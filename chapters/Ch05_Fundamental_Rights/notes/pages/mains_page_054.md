### Freedom of Press

- **1.** It is a cornerstone principle that ensures the right to freely communicate and express information through various media channels like print(newspaper), electronic(TV), social media(Twitter), etc.

- **2.** As per the World Press Freedom Index 2025, India ranks 151 out of 180 countries.

- **3.** Press and Registration of Periodicals Act became fully operational, replacing the 1867 colonial law

- **4.** There are over 1.54 lakh registered publications in India Key features and changes under the new Act include:

- Defines "periodical" to include registered newspapers, magazines, and journals, but excludes books and journals published by educational institutions.

- Office of PRGI: Replaces the Registrar of Newspapers for India (RNI), serving as the sole authority for title allotment and registration of periodicals, including newspapers and magazines.

- Registration is now a fully online process via the Press Sewa Portal, eliminating the need to make a declaration before a District Magistrate for starting a publication.

- Decriminalization: The act removes strict penalties (like imprisonment) that were present in the 1867 Act, focusing on penalties.

- Restrictions: Individuals convicted of terrorist acts, unlawful activities, or actions against national security are prohibited from publishing periodicals.

- Printing Presses: Printers only need to file an online intimation with the PRGI rather than a formal declaration.

### Constitutional Basis

- **1.** Not explicitly mentioned, but derived from Article 19(1)(a) - Freedom of Speech & Expression.

- **2.** Subject to reasonable restrictions under Article 19(2): sovereignty, security, public order, decency, morality, contempt of court, defamation, incitement to offence.

### Judicial Recognition

- **1.** Indian Express Newspapers v. Union of India (1985) - SC held press freedom is part of the basic structure of the Constitution.

- **2.** Sakal Papers v. Union of India (1962) - Government cannot restrict circulation of newspapers to control ideas.
