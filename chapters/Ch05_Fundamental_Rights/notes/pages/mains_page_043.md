### Challenges

Political

- **1.** Caste Census data may be weaponized for vote-bank politics, and could fuel sectarian populism.

- **2.** "Competitive Backwardness" - Fresh demographic weight encourages dominant communities to seek "backward" status, risking a "race to the bottom." Social

- **1.** Deepening of Caste Identities and Consciousness: "Enumeration is not a neutral act; it carries with it the risk of reifying the very categories it seeks to understand." - Dipankar Gupta

- **2.** Reification of Social Hierarchies - Scholars like M.N. Srinivas warned that overemphasis on caste in public discourse may harden caste boundaries and hinder social mobility. Legal

- **1.** New data showing higher backward populations will likely trigger legal challenges to the Supreme Court's 50% reservation cap. Eg. The 2023 Bihar Survey showed 63% OBC/EBCs Administrative

- **1.** Privacy and Data Misuse - Linking caste to digital IDs raises fears of surveillance and the potential misuse of data for targeted political exclusion.

- **2.** Administrative challenges: Caste is not uniform; identical groups use different names across states, making standardized "coding" nearly impossible.

- **3.** Misclassification or over-enumeration could create data reliability issues, as seen during the Socio-Economic and Caste Census (SECC) 2011, whose caste data was withheld due to inconsistencies.

### Way forward for advancing Equity Without Deepening Divisions

- **1.** Merging caste enumeration with data on education, income, health, sanitation, and land ownership, adopting a holistic lens rather than relying on caste alone.

- **2.** Establishing a multi-tier verification mechanism and a grievance redressal system for corrections.

- **3.** Ensure caste data is anonymized and used strictly for policymaking, not for political mobilisation.

- **4.** As per Suhas Palshikar, prepare a backwardness index based on the caste census and incentivize the inclusion of persons with a high backwardness index in private employment.

- **5.** Sub-categorization for Equity:

  - **a.** Implement Justice Rohini Commission's recommendations for sub-categorizing OBCs.

  - **b.** Aligning with the Supreme Court judgment in State of Punjab v. Davinder Singh (2024), sub-classify SCs and STs within the reservation quota.
