### SC Judgments

- **1.** Aligarh Muslim University (AMU) Case (2024): the SC overruled the 1967 S. Azeez Basha judgment (which claimed a university established by statute cannot be a minority institution).

  - **a.** Criteria for Minority Status:

  - **b.** Genesis and Purpose: The origin and objective of establishing the institution.

  - **c.** Founders and Contributors: Identification of individuals or entities who established and funded the institution.

  - **d.** Administrative Control: The extent of control exercised by the minority community over the institution's administration.

  - **e.** Pre-Constitution Institutions: The Court held that institutions established before the Constitution's commencement are also entitled to Article 30 protections.

- **2.** Kerala Education Bill (1958): right to "administer" does not include the right to "mismanage." It allowed the State to impose reasonable regulations (Eg. teacher qualifications, hygiene) to maintain standards without destroying the "minority character" of the institution.

- **3.** T.M.A. Pai Foundation v. State of Karnataka (2002): An 11-judge bench held that the unit for determining religious or linguistic minority status is the State, not the whole of India.

- **4.** St. Stephen's College -allowed minority institutions to reserve up to 50% of seats for own community.

- **5.** P.A. Inamdar v. State of Maharashtra (2005): The SC ruled that the State cannot impose its reservation policy (for SC/ST/OBC) on unaided private minority or non-minority colleges.
