### Statutory Regulations

- **1.** Press Council of India Act, 1978 - Establishes the PCI, a quasi-judicial body to uphold press ethics and prevent misuse of freedom.

- **2.** Registration of Newspapers (RNI) Act, 1956 - Regulates title verification, ownership, and circulation data.

- **3.** Cable Television Networks (Regulation) Act, 1995 - Controls content codes for electronic media.

- **4.** Information Technology Act, 2000 (Section 69A) - Empowers government to block online content.

### Institutional Mechanisms

- **1.** Press Council of India (PCI) - Advisory; can censure newspapers but lacks punitive power.

- **2.** News Broadcasters & Digital Association (NBDA) - Industry-led regulation for TV and digital news.

- **3.** Broadcasting Content Complaints Council (BCCC) - Regulates non-news entertainment content.

- **4.** Ministry of Information & Broadcasting (MIB) - Supervises press, broadcasting, and online content.

### Significance of Freedom of Press

- **1.** Democratic Oversight: Acts as a watchdog, exposing corruption and ensuring the government remains accountable to its citizens. Eg. Investigative reports on electoral bond data in 2024-25

- **2.** Check on Abuse of Power - Acts as a safeguard against authoritarian tendencies and arbitrary state actions. Eg. Investigative reports on Pegasus surveillance case (2021).

- **3.** Acts as a Bridge between State & Society - Serves as a medium for policy feedback and grievance redressal. Eg. Coverage of COVID-19 migrant workers' crisis (2020) prompted state action.

- **4.** Voice of Marginalized: Eg. women led digital news outlets like Khabar Lahariya highlight rural gender issues ignored by mainstream media.

- **5.** Checks Misinformation: Professional journalism counters the spread of "fake news" and viral AI deepfakes on social media. Eg. Alt fact check

- **6.** Judicial Support: Media coverage often prompts the judiciary to take suo motu cognizance of gross human rights violations.

- **7.** Economic Accountability: Financial journalism monitors corporate governance and protects retail investors from market fraud or manipulation. Eg. Harshad Metha scam expose

- **8.** Promotes Pluralism & Diversity - Enables debates on religion, caste, gender, and regional issues, reinforcing unity in diversity. Eg. Reporting on LGBTQ+ rights post-Navtej Johar (2018) case.

- **9.** Strengthens Rule of Law - Exposes violations of fundamental rights and mobilises opinion for legal reforms. Eg. Media role in Nirbhaya case (2012) leading to stricter rape laws.
