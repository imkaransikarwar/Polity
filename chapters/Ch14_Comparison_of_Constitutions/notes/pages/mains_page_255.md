### India- USA

### Similarities

- **1.** The Constitution is written and supreme, forming the highest law of the land.

- **2.** The system follows a federal structure dividing powers between the Union and States.

- **3.** The Constitution holds supremacy over all other laws and institutions.

- **4.** An independent judiciary safeguards constitutional provisions.

- **5.** The courts exercise judicial review to strike down unconstitutional laws.

- **6.** Judicial precedents guide legal interpretation and decision-making.

- **7.** Fundamental rights guarantee individual liberty and equality.

- **8.** Separation of powers - A written distribution of powers defines the authority of different government levels.

- **9.** A bicameral legislature functions through two representative chambers.

- **10.** The government is a republic with an elected head of state for a fixed term. The concept of popular sovereignty ensures ultimate authority lies with the people.

- **11.** The principle of rule of law and due process governs all actions of the State.
