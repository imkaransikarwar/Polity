| India | France |
| --- | --- |
| PM presides over Cabinet meetings. | President presides over the Council of Ministers (Conseil des Ministres). |
| Emergency powers are exercised on written Cabinet advice. | President has wide personal discretion during emergencies. |
| PM is responsible only to the Lok Sabha. | PM is responsible to both the President and Parliament. |

| Indian Secularism | French Model (Laïcité) |
| --- | --- |
| Positive secularism - equal respect for all religions | Negative secularism - strict separation of religion and State |
| State maintains a principled distance, engaging when necessary | State maintains complete separation and avoids any involvement |
| Public Expression Permitted (Eg. wearing religious symbols) | Prohibited in public institutions (Eg. veil ban in schools) |
| State may intervene to reform religion (Eg. social justice laws) | State non-interventionist, acts only to protect public order |
| Promotes religious harmony and equality among communities | Ensures religion-free public sphere and neutrality of the Republic |
| Recognizes religion as a social and cultural reality | Treats religion as a private and individual affair |
| Protects minority rights (Articles 29-30) | No special recognition for religious minorities |
