| --- | --- |
| Single Integrated Judiciary with one legal system across India. | UK SC interprets separate systems of England & Wales, Scotland, and Northern Ireland. |

| Under Article 143 President can seek SC's opinion. | No formal advisory role to the Monarch. |
| Liberal PIL System - relaxed locus standi allows any citizen to file for public interest. | Requires claimant to show sufficient interest in the matter. |
| Rights are entrenched under Part III of the Constitution. | Rights are mainly statutory under the Human Rights Act, 1998. |

### Parliamentary Privileges

| India | United Kingdom (UK) |
| --- | --- |
| Constitutional - Derived from Article 105. | Common Law & Custom - Based on Lex Parliamenti (Law and Custom of Parliament). |
| Courts can review existence of privilege and its compatibility with Fundamental Rights. Eg. Searchlight Case, Keshav Singh Case | Courts generally avoid interfering in parliamentary privileges. |
| Criminal Immunity - Earlier broader, now narrowing. Eg. Sita Soren Case | No immunity for criminal acts. |
| Expulsion of Members - Exists but subject to judicial review. | Inherent power of House of Commons, rarely challenged. |
