### India- UK

### Similarities

- **1.** The system operates under a parliamentary form of government with a collective responsibility of the executive to the legislature.

- **2.** The Cabinet system functions as the real center of political and administrative power.

- **3.** The Prime Minister is the head of government and leads the Council of Ministers.

- **4.** The President in India and the Monarch in the UK serve as nominal heads of state.

- **5.** The Parliament is the supreme law-making body consisting of two houses.

- **6.** The Council of Ministers is collectively responsible to the Lower House (Lok Sabha/House of Commons).

- **7.** The Rule of Law governs all citizens and authorities, ensuring equality before law.

- **8.** The parliamentary privileges of members are recognized and protected.

- **9.** The bicameral legislature structure includes an upper and lower chamber.

- **10.** The parliamentary procedure and traditions in India are modeled on Westminster practices.

- **11.** The Cabinet Secretariat and civil service neutrality are features inherited from the British model.
