### India- France

### Similarities

- **1.** Both are written constitutions serving as the supreme law of the land.

- **2.** Both declare the State as a sovereign, democratic, and secular republic.

- **3.** Both emphasize the principles of liberty, equality, and fraternity in the Preamble.

- **4.** Both establish a Republican form of government with an elected head of state.

- **5.** Both provide for a bicameral legislature at the national level.

- **6.** Both allow emergency provisions empowering the executive during crises.

- **7.** Both stress constitutional supremacy over all organs of the State

### Executive

### Similarities

- **1.** Dual Executive Structure: a Head of State (President) and a Head of Government (PM).

- **2.** In both systems, the Prime Minister is the primary official responsible for the legislative agenda and government policy.

- **3.** Both the Indian and French Presidents hold the supreme command of the armed forces.

- **4.** Pardoning Powers

- **5.** The Indian President issues Ordinances (Article 123) during recess, while the French President can issue Decrees in the Council of Ministers.
