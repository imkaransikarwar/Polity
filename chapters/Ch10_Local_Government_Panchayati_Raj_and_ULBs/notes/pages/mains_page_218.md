### Municipal Bonds

Municipal Bonds are debt instruments issued by Urban Local Bodies or municipal corporations to raise funds from the open market for specific infrastructure projects. Recent News

- Budget 2026-27: ₹100 crore incentive for any single municipal bond issuance exceeding ₹1,000 crore. This aims to encourage large-scale "City Economic Region" projects.

- RBI Repo Guideline (Oct 2025): allowed municipal bonds to be used as collateral for repo transactions.

- Urban Challenge Fund (UCF): requires cities to raise at least 50% of project costs via municipal bonds or private loans. Key Data Points

- Market Size: Total outstanding municipal debt reached ₹13,500 crore by March 2025, with FY26 issuances projected to hit ₹2,000 crore.

- Credit Quality: Nearly 32 major Indian cities now hold "Investment Grade" ratings.

- The U.S. muni market is 14% of its GDP, while India's remains at 0.06% of GDP in 2026.

### Success Case Studies

- **1.** Indore: Raised ₹244 crore (oversubscribed 6x) to fund a 60MW solar power plant.

- **2.** Pimpri-Chinchwad: Leveraged its AA+ rating to raise funds for smart-city digital infrastructure and fiber-grid networks.
