### ELECTIONS, RESERVATION AND TENURE

| FEATURE / PARAMETER | RURAL LOCAL SELF GOVERNMENT (73RD AMENDMENT) | URBAN LOCAL SELF GOVERNMENT (74TH AMENDMENT) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| State Election Commission | **1.** Mandatory: Conducts electoral rolls and all Panchayat elections (Art 243K).<br>**2.** The State Election Commissioner is appointed by the Governor, who fixes his service conditions and tenure.<br>**3.** Removal only like a High Court Judge; service conditions cannot be worsened after appointment.<br>**4.** Other election matters are left to State law. | Same principle governs. The same Commission conducts municipal rolls and elections (Art 243ZA). |
| Qualifications and disqualifications [PRELIMS 2016] | **1.** Same qualifications as for the State Legislature, but the minimum age is 21, not 25 (Art 243F).<br>**2.** Disqualification is under State law; disputes go to an authority set by State law. | Same principle governs. Minimum age 21 (Art 243V). |
| Reservation of seats and offices | **1.** Mandatory: Seats for SCs and STs at all levels, in proportion to their population (Art 243D(1)).<br>**2.** Mandatory: At least one third of seats for women, including SC/ST women’s seats (Art 243D(2), (3)).<br>**3.** Mandatory: At least one third of chairperson posts at each level for women (Art 243D(4)).<br>**4.** Mandatory: State law reserves chairperson posts for SCs and STs (Art 243D(4)).<br>**5.** Discretionary: Reservation for backward classes (Art 243D(6)).<br>**6.** SC/ST reservation, not women’s, ends with the Art 334 period, now 25 January 2030 under the 104th Amendment, 2019 (Art 243D(5)).<br>**7.** No SC reservation in Arunachal Pradesh, which has no SC population (83rd Amendment, 2000; Art 243M). | Same principle governs. (Art 243T) No one third floor for women chairpersons; chairperson posts are reserved as State law provides (Art 243T(4)). |
| Duration and dissolution [PRELIMS 2009] | **1.** Five year term from the date of the first meeting, unless dissolved earlier (Art 243E(1)).<br>**2.** Elections must be held before the term ends, and within six months of an early dissolution (Art 243E(3)).<br>**3.** The new body serves only the remainder of the term.<br>**4.** No election is needed if the remainder is under six months.<br>**5.** Amending a law cannot dissolve a Panchayat before its term ends (Art 243E(2)). | Same principle governs. (Art 243U) A Municipality must get a reasonable opportunity of being heard before dissolution (Art 243U(1)). |
