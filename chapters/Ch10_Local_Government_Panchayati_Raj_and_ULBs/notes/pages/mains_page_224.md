### Way Forward

- **1.** 125th Amendment Bill: Fast-track this bill to introduce Village Councils (similar to Gram Sabhas) and make the Anti-Defection law applicable to ADCs.

- **2.** Mandate the Central Finance Commission to directly allocate funds to ADCs, bypassing state discretion (as per the B.K. Chaturvedi Committee).

- **3.** Amend the Schedule to provide at least 33% reservation for women.

- **4.** Activity Mapping: Clearly delineate functions between the State, District, and Village levels
