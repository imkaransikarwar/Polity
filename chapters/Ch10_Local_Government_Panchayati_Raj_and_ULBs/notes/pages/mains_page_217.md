### Way Forward

- **1.** Move toward a "Mayor in council" system with executive powers and a fixed 5-year tenure (similar to London or New York).

- **2.** Financial Autonomy: Implement the FC recommendation of 5% annual OSR growth and leverage the ₹1 lakh crore Urban Challenge Fund for market borrowing.

- **3.** Institutionalizing "Value Capture Financing" (VCF) to monetize public infrastructure investments. Eg. Betterment Levies or higher FSI charges on lands adjacent to Metro line

- **4.** Create a Dedicated Urban Planning Cadre (as suggested by the HPEC on Urban Infrastructure) to professionalize municipal administration.
