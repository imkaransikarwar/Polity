### Government Initiatives

- **1.** Revamped Rashtriya Gram Swaraj Abhiyan (RGSA) - Capacity building and training of over 31 lakh elected representatives and PRI functionaries.

  - **a.** Training on 9 thematic areas such as "Healthy Village," "Water Sufficient Village," and "Women Friendly Village."

  - **b.** Infrastructure: Funding for the construction of Gram Panchayat Bhawans and co-location of Common Service Centres (CSCs).

  - **c.** In 2025-26, a specialized module for Women Elected Representatives (WER) was launched to reduce "Sarpanch-Pati" culture.

### Way Forward

- **1.** Launch a new decentralisation drive (Panchayati Raj 2.0) to revive local governance momentum.

- **2.** Second Administrative Reforms Commission (ARC) - 6th Report on Local Governance (2007)

  - **a.** Activity Mapping: Clearly define and notify the functions of each tier of PRIs based on the principle of subsidiarity.

  - **b.** Strengthen District Planning Committees (DPCs): Make them functional with genuine planning power and integration across rural-urban lines.

  - **c.** Capacity Building: Establish State Institutes of Rural Development (SIRDs) as nodal agencies for PRI training.

  - **d.** Performance Audits: Institute independent performance audits of PRIs regularly.

  - **e.** Social Audit: Institutionalize social audits in all developmental schemes implemented through PRIs.

  - **f.** Smaller, unviable Panchayats should be merged for better administrative efficiency.

- **3.** Mani Shankar Aiyar Committee (2013)

  - **a.** No project should be implemented without prior informed consent of Gram Sabha

  - **b.** Called for the abolition or integration of state-run parastatals (like Water Boards)

  - **c.** Establish National Commission for Panchayati Raj.
