### CONSTITUTIONAL SCHEME

| FEATURE / PARAMETER | RURAL LOCAL SELF GOVERNMENT (73RD AMENDMENT) | URBAN LOCAL SELF GOVERNMENT (74TH AMENDMENT) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Constitutional basis and nature of local self-government [PRELIMS 2017,2015,2011] | **1.** The 73rd Amendment Act, 1992 inserted Part IX and the Eleventh Schedule of 29 functional items (Arts 243 to 243O).<br>**2.** Local self government means democratic decentralisation: power, funds and functions move to elected village, block and district bodies.<br>**3.** Panchayats draw their powers from State law.<br>**4.** The system is representative; only the Gram Sabha is directly democratic.<br>**5.** The core objective is people’s participation in development; financial mobilisation and accountability are only the means.<br>**6.** The State shall organise village panchayats as units of self government (Art 40, DPSP). | Same principle governs. The 74th Amendment Act, 1992 inserted Part IXA and the Twelfth Schedule of 18 matters (Arts 243P to 243ZG). No Directive Principle like Art 40 for urban bodies. |
