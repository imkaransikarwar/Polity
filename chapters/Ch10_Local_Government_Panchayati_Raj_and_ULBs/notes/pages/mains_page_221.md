### Major Issues/Challenges

- **1.** Executive-Legislative Deadlock

- **2.** Ceremonial Nature of Post: In many states, even directly elected mayors lack actual "executive powers," which remain with the Commissioner.

- **3.** Conflict with State Government in funds

- **4.** Tussle with MPs and MLAs: Directly elected can be seen as political rivals to local legislators, leading to administrative friction.

- **5.** Risk of Autocratic Behavior without strong "Mayor-in-Council" checks.

- **6.** Increased Election Costs

- **7.** Populist Bias

### Way Forward

- **1.** Strong Mayor-in-Council Model - Eg. Kolkata

- **2.** 2nd ARC Recommendation: Mayor should be Executive Head of the city with a fixed tenure.

- **3.** Global Best Practice (London/New York): "Executive Mayor" system where the Mayor proposes the budget and the Council performs the audit/veto function.

- **4.** Dedicated Municipal Cadre, independent of state-level transfers.

- **5.** Introduce a "Right to Recall" to ensure the Mayor remains responsive throughout the term.

- **6.** Direct a fixed percentage of State GST (S-GST) to the Mayor's office to ensure financial autonomy from the state.

- **7.** Form a "City Coordination Committee" chaired by the Mayor, including local MPs/MLAs, to prevent jurisdictional friction.

### Delays in elections of local bodies

After nearly four years of delay, elections for the Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation (BMC) and other major cities were finally held in January 2026.

- The average delay in municipal elections across major Indian states stands at 22 to 24 months after the expiry of the previous council's term.

- Over 30% of urban residents in India are currently governed by state-appointed administrators

### Constitutional Provisions

- **1.** Article 243-E (Panchayats) & 243-U (Municipalities): Mandate a fixed term of five years. Elections must be completed before the expiry of this term.

- **2.** Six-Month Rule: In case of dissolution, elections must be held within six months from the date of such dissolution.

- **3.** Article 243-K & 243-ZA: Empower the State Election Commission with the "superintendence, direction, and control" of conduct of all local body elections.

### Reasons Behind Delays

- **1.** OBC Reservation Litigation: States struggle to fulfill the SC-mandated "Triple Test" (Commission, Empirical Data, 50% Ceiling) for backward class quotas.

- **2.** Delimitation Exercises: State governments use the redrawing of ward boundaries as a tactical tool to postpone polls.
