### Difference Between Council of Ministers and Cabinet

| Aspect | Council of Ministers | Cabinet |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Constitutional Mention | Mentioned in Article 74 & 75 of the Constitution | Not explicitly mentioned, but exists as the core decision-making body |
| Size | Larger body - includes all categories of ministers (Cabinet Ministers, Ministers of State, and Deputy Ministers) | Smaller body - includes only senior Cabinet Ministers |
| Composition | - Cabinet Ministers - Ministers of State (Independent charge & attached) - Deputy Ministers | Only Cabinet Ministers |
| Meetings | Rarely meets as a full body; decisions are taken by Cabinet or individual ministers | Meets regularly to discuss and decide on government policy and administration |
| Decision-making power | Collectively responsible to Lok Sabha, but real decisions are usually taken by the Cabinet | Real executive authority; its decisions bind the entire Council of Ministers |

### Cabinet

The Cabinet is the core of the Council of Ministers and the highest decision-making authority in the Indian politico- administrative system. It was not included in the original text of the Constitution and the 44th Constitutional Amendment Act,1978 inserted 'Cabinet 'into Article 352 of the Constitution.

### Significance of the Cabinet System in India

- **1.** Real Centre of Executive Power - It drives national policy, handles administrative affairs, and directs all ministries and departments.

- **2.** Principle of Division of Labour and effective delegation - Enhances specialization, expertise, and sectoral focus within governance.

- **3.** Supreme Policy Formulation: It serves as the highest authority for determining national priorities and long-term strategic goals.

- **4.** Inter-Ministerial Coordination: It resolves jurisdictional overlaps between different ministries to ensure a unified "Whole of Government" approach. Eg. PM Gati Shakti

- **5.** All major government bills and constitutional amendments must receive Cabinet approval before being introduced in Parliament.

- **6.** Financial Oversight: The Cabinet approves the Union Budget and all major public expenditure proposals exceeding specific fiscal thresholds.

- **7.** Binding Advice to President: Under Article 74, its advice is binding, making it the de facto wielder of the President's powers.

- **8.** It determines India's stance on international treaties, global alliances, and strategic defense agreements.
