### Way Forward: Ensuring Constitutional Discipline and Cooperative Federalism

- **1.** Codify Timelines into Law or Constitutional Amendment

- **2.** Define and Limit Governor's Discretion Constitutionally

- **3.** Create a parliamentary or judicial oversight mechanism to track delays in grant of assent.

- **4.** Introduce a Code of Conduct for Governors, linked with a review mechanism by the Inter-State Council or Rajya Sabha.

- **5.** Frame guidelines for future use of Article 142 to prevent charges of judicial overreach.

- **6.** Institutionalize regular Centre-State consultations (through NITI Aayog or Inter-State Council) on issues affecting federal relations.

### Constitutional Provisions

- **1.** Article 74: Council of Ministers to aid and advise the President. This advice is binding on the President after the 44th Amendment to the Constitution.

- **2.** Article 75: Composition of the Council of Ministers; their appointment, tenure, responsibility, etc.

- **3.** Article 77: Conduct of business of the Government of India.

- **4.** Article 88: Ministers can speak and participate in either House of Parliament, joint sittings, and committees, but cannot vote.

- **5.** 91st Amendment Act, 2003: Limited the size of the Council of Ministers to 15% of Lok Sabha or state assembly members, to prevent excessive appointments.

### Powers and Functions

- **1.** Collective Responsibility: Under Article 75(3), the Council is collectively accountable to the Lok Sabha for all executive decisions.

- **2.** Formulation of National Policy: The Council deliberates on and decides all major domestic and foreign policies for the nation.

- **3.** Aiding and Advising the President: Under Article 74, the Council provides binding advice to the President on all executive functions.

- **4.** Legislative Leadership: Ministers draft and introduce almost all major government bills and steer them through both Houses.

- **5.** Financial Management and Budgeting: The Council is responsible for preparing the Union Budget and ensuring fiscal discipline across ministries.

- **6.** Supreme Executive Control: Each minister oversees the administration of their assigned department, ensuring the implementation of government programs.

- **7.** Coordination of Administration: The Council ensures synergy between different departments to avoid policy conflicts and overlapping jurisdictions.
