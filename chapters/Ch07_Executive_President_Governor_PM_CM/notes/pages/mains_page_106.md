### Issues

- **1.** Concentration of Power in PMO - PMO has expanded into a "parallel government" (Subhash Kashyap). Eg. COVID-19 lockdown decisions centralised.

- **2.** Erosion of Cabinet Collective Responsibility - Eg. 1975 emergency declaration bypassed full Cabinet.

- **3.** Presidentialisation of PM - Shift towards "prime ministerial government". Eg. in foreign policy decisions.

- **4.** Marginalisation of Parliament - Eg. Farm Laws (2020) passed with limited debate.

- **5.** Coalition vs. Majority Dilemma - PM's role weakened in coalitions but over-centralised in majority regimes.

- **6.** Opacity in Decision-Making - Eg. PM CARES Fund not under parliamentary oversight.

- **7.** Institutional Weakening - PMO dominance sidelines institutions. Eg. NITI Aayog reduced to advisory role.

### Way Forward

- **1.** Introduce a PM's Question Hour (UK Model) to improve direct accountability.

- **2.** Restoring Collective Cabinet Responsibility - Define clear guidelines to prevent the PMO from bypassing ministers and functioning as a super-executive.

- **3.** Internal Party Democracy: PMs must emerge from transparent intra-party elections.

- **4.** Bring PM CARES under RTI: Ensure financial transparency.

- **5.** Appointment of key constitutional officers (CAG, CEC, etc.) should follow the collegium model involving CJI, Leader of Opposition, and PM.

- **6.** Codification of Conventions - Draw from UK's Cabinet Manual, particularly concerning PM's powers, Cabinet procedures, and PMO functioning.

### PMO

### Origin

- **1.** 1947–1977: Established as the Prime Minister's Secretariat (PMS) to provide secretarial assistance to Jawaharlal Nehru.

- **2.** 1977: Renamed the Prime Minister's Office (PMO) during the tenure of Morarji Desai to project a less "secretive" and more "office-like" image.

- **3.** Evolution: Under Lal Bahadur Shastri, the role of the Principal Secretary was created, shifting the PMO from a mere clerical body to a high-level policy advisory unit.

### Key Functions

- **1.** Secretarial Assistance: Provides the PM with technical and administrative support for managing the Union Cabinet and Parliament.

- **2.** Inter-Ministerial Coordination: resolving inter-departmental disputes and ensuring policy synergy.

- **3.** Policy Monitoring: Tracks the progress of "big-ticket" infrastructure projects (via the Project Monitoring Group) and flagship schemes like Gati Shakti.

- **4.** National Security & Foreign Policy: Houses the National Security Advisor (NSA) and coordinates with intelligence agencies and the Ministry of External Affairs.

- **5.** Public Grievances: Manages the CPGRAMS portal and the Prime Minister's National Relief Fund (PMNRF).
