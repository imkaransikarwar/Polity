| Impeachment[P RELIMS 2013] | **1.** The procedure has both political and quasi-judicial characteristics.<br>**2.** The only ground for impeachment is violation of the Constitution.<br>**3.** The charge may be initiated in either House of Parliament.<br>**4.** The charge is preferred by a resolution of the House.<br>**5.** The resolution can be moved only after 14 days’ written notice, signed by not less than 1/4 of the total membership of that House.<br>**6.** The initiating House must pass the resolution by a majority of not less than 2/3 of the total membership of that House.<br>**7.** The charge is then investigated by the other House.<br>**8.** During the investigation, the President has the right to appear and to be represented.<br>**9.** If, after investigation, the other House sustains the charge by a majority of not less than 2/3 of its total membership, the President is removed from office.<br>**10.** Thus, both Houses are involved, but they perform different functions: the first House prefers the charge; the other House investigates and, if it sustains the charge with the required majority, the President is removed. | **1.** No formal process of impeachment.<br>**2.** The Governor holds the office during the pleasure of the President. |
