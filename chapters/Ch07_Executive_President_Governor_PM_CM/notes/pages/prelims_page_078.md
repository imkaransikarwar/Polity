| **Cabinet Committees** *(Contd.)* | **5.** Eight standing Cabinet Committees (as reconstituted in 2019):<br>**(a)** Appointments (ACC) and Accommodation.<br>**(b)** Security, Political Affairs, Parliamentary Affairs.<br>**(c)** Economic Affairs, Investment and Growth, Employment and Skill Development.<br>**6.** Their decisions may be reviewed by the full Cabinet but in practice stand as Cabinet decisions. TRAP<br>**1.** The ACC - which clears senior civil service appointments - has just two members, the Prime Minister and the Home Minister (the Minister concerned is consulted for his own department).<br>**2.** It is not chaired by the Cabinet Secretary, and it is distinct from the Civil Services Board, which the Cabinet Secretary heads.<br>**3.** Distinguish both from Groups of Ministers (GoMs), which are ad hoc and dissolved once their task is done. | — |
