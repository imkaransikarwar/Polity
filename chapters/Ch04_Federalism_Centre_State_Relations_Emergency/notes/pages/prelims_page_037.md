| PARAMETER | FEDERAL FEATURES | UNITARY FEATURES | TRAP |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Independent machinery | Constitutional bodies insulated from both levels. | A single Election Commission and a single CAG serve the Union and the States alike. | No separate State Election Commission for parliamentary or assembly elections. Art 243K creates one only for local bodies. |
| Territorial integrity | None. States have no guaranteed territory. | Art 3 lets Parliament alter the area, boundaries or name of any State by a simple majority, on the President's recommendation, after merely referring the Bill to the State legislature for its views. | The State legislature's view is not binding, and there is no time limit binding Parliament to it. |
| Emergency | In normal times the two levels operate in their own spheres. | Under Arts 352, 356 and 360 the Constitution turns unitary, with Parliament legislating on the State List and the Centre directing the States. | K.C. Wheare called India quasi federal; Granville Austin called it cooperative federalism. The Constitution itself never uses the word federation. |
