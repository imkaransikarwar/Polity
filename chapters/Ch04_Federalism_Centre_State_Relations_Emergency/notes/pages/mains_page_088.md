### Issues

- **1.** Political

  - **a.** Politicization of office of Governor - Eg. Delays in assent to bills in Tamil Nadu, Kerala

  - **b.** Bargaining federalism (Morris Jones) - Different ruling parties at Centre & States fuel confrontation. Eg. West Bengal boycotting NITI Aayog meetings.

  - **c.** Policy Imposition - Eg. Three language formula under NEP,2020.

  - **d.** Centralized Decision-Making - Eg. COVID-19 guidelines under Disaster Management Act, 2005.

- **2.** Legal-Institutional

  - **a.** Overlapping Jurisdictions - Conflict over Concurrent List subjects. Eg. Farm Laws (2020) challenged by states.

  - **b.** NITI Aayog's Limited Power - Advisory nature weakens cooperative policy-making.

  - **c.** Emergency Provisions Misuse - Article 356 is sometimes used for political gains, undermining state autonomy.

  - **d.** Officer Transfers & Postings - Disputes over cadre officer deputations. Eg. West Bengal IPS officer recall issue.

  - **e.** Misuse of Central Agencies - Eg. CBI consent revoked by opposition-led states.

  - **f.** Lack of Institutions for Federal Communication - Inter-State Council remains ineffective as it has no binding authority.

- **3.** Financial

  - **a.** GST Council Disputes - States allege loss of fiscal autonomy & delayed compensation.

  - **b.** Centralisation via Finance - Cuts in central funding for schemes burden states. Eg. Recent MGNREGA changes

  - **c.** Delayed Fiscal Transfers - Eg. GST compensation cess delays (2019-22).
