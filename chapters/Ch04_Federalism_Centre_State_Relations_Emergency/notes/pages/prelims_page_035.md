## CHAPTER 04: UNITARY VS FEDERAL

### THEME ANALYSIS PYQ pattern for this chapter

#### 1. REPEATS

#### A. The Essential Federal Marker

- The one essential federal feature tested is a safeguarded independent judiciary, umpiring Centre and State disputes under Art 131 [2021].

- Indian federalism is not the result of an agreement among federating units; Ambedkar called it an indestructible Union of destructible States [2017].

- Unequal State representation in the Rajya Sabha is Indian, unlike the equal American Senate; equal representation is the classic federal test India fails [2017].

#### B. What Makes the Polity Federal

- Dual polity: Union and State governments, each drawing authority directly from the Constitution.

- Three lists in the Seventh Schedule; a written, largely rigid and supreme Constitution, with inconsistent law void under Art 13.

- Bicameralism, with the Rajya Sabha as the chamber representing the States.

#### C. The Unitary Tilt

- Residuary powers rest with the Union; Parliament reaches the State List under Arts 249, 250, 252, 253 and during Art 356.

- Single citizenship, a single integrated judiciary, one Election Commission and one CAG for the Union and the States.

- All India Services under Art 312; the Governor appointed by the Centre and holding office during the President's pleasure.

- Art 3: Parliament alters the area, boundaries or name of a State by simple majority; no State has guaranteed territory.

#### D. Labels and Comparisons

- K.C. Wheare: quasi federal; Granville Austin: cooperative federalism; the Constitution never uses the word federation.

- Under Arts 352, 356 and 360 the Constitution turns unitary, with the Centre directing the States.

- Residuary power lies with the States in the USA and Australia, with the Centre in Canada and India; the USA has a dual judiciary and dual citizenship.

#### 2. HOW THE FORMAT HAS CHANGED

- 2017: a single answer "not a feature" stem on Indian federalism [2017]

- 2021: a single answer on the one essential feature that makes the polity federal [2021]

#### 3. WHAT IS BEING ASKED NOW

- Federal character tested through the one essential feature, an independent judiciary [2021]

- Sorting features as federal or unitary: Seventh Schedule, Rajya Sabha composition, single citizenship, All India Services and Art 3
