### Way Forward for federalism

- **1.** Sarkaria & Punchhi Commissions:

  - **a.** Governors should be eminent persons from outside the state, not active politicians from the ruling central party.

  - **b.** Implement a fixed 5-year tenure for Governors, with removal only through a process similar to impeachment rather than "at the pleasure of the President."

- **2.** Inter-State Council (Article 263) Reforms:

  - **a.** Mandatory Meetings at least three times a year (as recommended by NCRWC).

  - **b.** Pre-Legislative Consultation with states through the Council before introducing any bill in Parliament concerning "Concurrent List" subjects.

- **3.** Strengthen the role of Rajya Sabha similar to German Bundesrat, where state governments have a direct and weighted vote on federal laws affecting regional administration.

- **4.** Move residuary powers of legislation from the Union List to the Concurrent List to give states more flexibility in emerging areas like AI and Digital Governance.

- **5.** Asymmetric Delimitation: use a "weighted formula" that rewards states for social milestones like health and education, instead of relying solely on population

- **6.** Fiscal Federalism Roadmap

  - **a.** Cess and Surcharge Reform: cap at 10% of Gross Tax Revenue to ensure the "divisible pool" for states is not artificially shrunk.

  - **b.** Flexible CSS: Allow states to customize 25–30% of the parameters of Centrally Sponsored Schemes (CSS) to suit local geographical and ecological needs

### Office of Governor

> "The Governor is a constitutional head and the custodian of the Constitution within the state." - Ambedkar "He serves as a bridge, ensuring that the dual system of governance works in harmony within the constitutional framework." - S. Radhakrishnan

### Powers and Functions of the Governor

The Governor's role is similar to the President of India, but without diplomatic, military, or emergency powers. Executive Powers

- **1.** Appoints the Chief Minister(Article 164), Ministers, Advocate General(Article 165), State Election Commissioner(Article 243K), and Public Service Commission members(Article 316).

- **2.** Oversees state executive actions and serves as Chancellor of state universities.

- **3.** Can recommend President's Rule(Article 356) and act as the Centre's representative during emergencies. Legislative Powers

- **1.** Summons, prorogues, and addresses the legislature(Article 174).

- **2.** Appoints presiding officers and nominated members(Article 171).

- **3.** Can exercise veto over bills by assenting, withholding, returning, or reserving them for the President(Article 200). Financial Powers

- **1.** Supervises the state budget and approves money bills(Article 199).

- **2.** Can make advances from the Contingency Fund(Article 267).
