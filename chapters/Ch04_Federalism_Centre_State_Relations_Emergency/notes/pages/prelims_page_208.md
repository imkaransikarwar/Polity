## CHAPTER 25: FEDERAL BODIES

### THEME ANALYSIS PYQ pattern for this chapter

#### 1. REPEATS

#### A. Tricky Statements and Subtle Distinctions

- Zonal Councils are statutory creations of the States Reorganisation Act, 1956, not constitutional bodies.

- The Inter State Council has a constitutional base in Art 263 yet was set up by a Presidential order, on the Sarkaria Commission's recommendation.

- The GST Council is the only constitutional body of the three, created by Art 279A.

- The North Eastern Council is statutory, under the North Eastern Council Act, 1971, and is not one of the five Zonal Councils [2024].

#### B. Type and Legal Basis at a Glance

- Art 279A created the GST Council through the 101st Amendment Act, 2016.

- The Union Home Minister chairs all five Zonal Councils, with a Chief Minister as rotating Vice Chairman.

- The North Eastern Council is a separate statutory body and is not one of the five Zonal Councils.

- NITI Aayog rests only on a Cabinet resolution of 1 January 2015, as the Planning Commission rested on a 1950 resolution [2015].

#### C. Decision Making and Voting Arithmetic

- A GST Council decision needs three fourths of the weighted votes of members present and voting.

- The Centre holds one third of that weight and all States together two thirds.

- The Inter State Council and Zonal Councils only advise and take no binding vote.

- NITI Aayog and the NEC are both advisory - neither allocates funds nor issues binding orders.

#### D. Who Chairs and Who Sits

- Prime Minister for the Inter State Council, Union Home Minister for the Zonal Councils, Union Finance Minister for the GST Council.

- The GST Council includes the Union Minister of State for Finance and a minister nominated by each State.

- Art 263 lets the President define the Council's duties, so its mandate rests on the order, not on the Article alone.

- The NEC comprises the Governor and Chief Minister of each of the eight States plus three Members nominated by the President; the Home Minister chairs it by a 2018 Cabinet decision, not by the 2002 amendment [2024].

#### E. Bodies Not Mentioned in the Constitution

- Zonal Councils, the Planning Commission and the National Development Council all find no mention in the Constitution [2013].

- Only the Inter-State Council among the coordination bodies has a constitutional source, Art 263 [2025].

- The NDC comprised the Prime Minister, Union Cabinet Ministers, Chief Ministers and members of the Planning Commission, never the Chairman of the Finance Commission [2013].

- Also tested: NDC status, planning in the Concurrent List, Panchayat plans [2013]

#### 2. HOW THE FORMAT HAS CHANGED

- 2013: three code sets on the NDC and bodies outside the Constitution [2013]

- 2015: a single answer stem on NITI Aayog [2015]

- 2024 to 2025: a composition set and a "how many" status count [2024, 2025]

#### 3. WHAT IS BEING ASKED NOW

- Legal status: constitutional (Inter State Council, Art 263) versus executive or statutory [2025]

- Membership: the NEC after 2002 [2024]
