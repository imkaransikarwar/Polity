## CHAPTER 04
UNITARY VS FEDERAL
THEME ANALYSIS PYQ pattern for this chapter
1. REPEATS

#### A. The Essential Federal Marker
- The one essential federal feature tested is a safeguarded independent judiciary, umpiring Centre and State disputes
under Art 131 [2021].
- Indian federalism is not the result of an agreement among federating units; Ambedkar called it an indestructible
Union of destructible States [2017].
- Unequal State representation in the Rajya Sabha is Indian, unlike the equal American Senate; equal representation is
the classic federal test India fails [2017].

#### B. What Makes the Polity Federal
- Dual polity: Union and State governments, each drawing authority directly from the Constitution.
- Three lists in the Seventh Schedule; a written, largely rigid and supreme Constitution, with inconsistent law void under
Art 13.
- Bicameralism, with the Rajya Sabha as the chamber representing the States.

#### C. The Unitary Tilt
- Residuary powers rest with the Union; Parliament reaches the State List under Arts 249, 250, 252, 253 and during Art
356.
- Single citizenship, a single integrated judiciary, one Election Commission and one CAG for the Union and the States.
- All India Services under Art 312; the Governor appointed by the Centre and holding office during the President's
pleasure.
- Art 3: Parliament alters the area, boundaries or name of a State by simple majority; no State has guaranteed territory.

#### D. Labels and Comparisons
- K.C. Wheare: quasi federal; Granville Austin: cooperative federalism; the Constitution never uses the word federation.
- Under Arts 352, 356 and 360 the Constitution turns unitary, with the Centre directing the States.
- Residuary power lies with the States in the USA and Australia, with the Centre in Canada and India; the USA has a dual
judiciary and dual citizenship.
2. HOW THE FORMAT HAS CHANGED
- 2017: a single answer "not a feature" stem on Indian federalism [2017]
- 2021: a single answer on the one essential feature that makes the polity federal [2021]
3. WHAT IS BEING ASKED NOW
- Federal character tested through the one essential feature, an independent judiciary [2021]
- Sorting features as federal or unitary: Seventh Schedule, Rajya Sabha composition, single citizenship, All India
Services and Art 3

### PRELIMS PREVIOUS YEAR QUESTIONS

**Q1. Which one of the following in Indian polity is an**
essential feature that indicates that it is federal in
character? [2021]
(a) The independence of the judiciary is safeguarded.
(b) The Union Legislature has elected
representatives from constituent units.
(c) The Union Cabinet can have elected
representatives from regional parties.
(d) The Fundamental Rights are enforceable by
Courts of Law.
**`ANSWER (a)`**

**Q2. Which one of the following is not a feature of Indian**
federalism ? [2017]
(a) There is an independent judiciary in India.
(b) Powers have been clearly divided between the
Centre and the States.
(c) The federating units have been given unequal
representation in the Rajya Sabha.
(d) It is the result of an agreement among the
federating units.
**`ANSWER (d)`**

Distribution of

In the USA and Australia the

States; in Canada, as in India,
with the Centre.
The

Written and largely rigid,

inconsistent with it is void
under Art 13.
Most of it can be amended by
Parliament alone; only the entries in
the proviso to Art 368(2) need
ratification by half the States.
A blend of rigidity and
flexibility, unlike the uniformly
rigid American Constitution.
Judiciary An independent judiciary
headed by the Supreme
Court umpires Centre and
State disputes under Art
131, the essential federal
indicator.
A single integrated judiciary
enforcing both Union and State laws,
with judges appointed by the Centre.
The USA has a dual judiciary,
federal and State. India
deliberately did not.
Legislature Bicameralism, with the
Rajya Sabha as the chamber
representing the States.
Representation is unequal, in
proportion to population under the
Fourth Schedule, and members are
elected by State legislators, not by the
people.
The US Senate gives two seats
to every State regardless of
size. Equal representation of
units is the classic federal test
India fails.
Citizenship None. A federal marker India
chose not to adopt.
Single citizenship for the whole
country.
The USA, Switzerland and
Australia have dual citizenship,
federal and State.
Executive of
the unit
The State government is
elected and responsible to
its own legislature.
The Governor is appointed by the
Centre and holds office during the
pleasure of the President (Art 155,
156).
The Governor doubles as the
agent of the Centre, which is
why Art 356 works through him.
Public services State Public Service
Commissions and State
services.
All India Services (IAS, IPS, IFoS) are
common to the Centre and the States
but controlled by the Centre (Art
312).
Officers serve the States but the
Centre holds disciplinary and
cadre control.

Independent
machinery
Constitutional bodies
insulated from both levels.
A single Election Commission and a
single CAG serve the Union and the
States alike.
No separate State Election
Commission for parliamentary or
assembly elections. Art 243K
creates one only for local bodies.
Territorial
integrity
None. States have no
guaranteed territory.
Art 3 lets Parliament alter the area,
boundaries or name of any State by a
simple majority, on the President's
recommendation, after merely
referring the Bill to the State
legislature for its views.
The State legislature's view is
not binding, and there is no time
limit binding Parliament to it.
Emergency In normal times the two
levels operate in their own
spheres.
Under Arts 352, 356 and 360 the
Constitution turns unitary, with
Parliament legislating on the State List
and the Centre directing the States.
K.C. Wheare called India quasi
federal; Granville Austin called
it cooperative federalism. The
Constitution itself never uses
the word federation.
