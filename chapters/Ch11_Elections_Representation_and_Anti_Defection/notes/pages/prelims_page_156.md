The 2026 delimitation package

- The Constitution (131st Amendment) Bill, 2026, the Delimitation Bill and the Union Territories Laws (Amendment) Bill were introduced on 16 April 2026: to lift the freeze, let Parliament decide by law when delimitation is done and on which census, and raise the Lok Sabha ceiling from 550 to 850 (States 530 to 815, UTs 20 to 35).

- Defeated on 17 April 2026: 298 of 528 present and voting, short of the two thirds required by Art 368(2); 230 against. The Delimitation Bill was then withdrawn. Traps

- The freeze until the first census after 2026 still stands.
