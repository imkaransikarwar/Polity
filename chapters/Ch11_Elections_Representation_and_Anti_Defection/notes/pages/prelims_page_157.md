Traps

- Registration is not voting: a convict stays an elector, so he can propose a candidate and contest (subject to Sec 8), but cannot vote while confined.

- A non member Minister can speak in either House (Arts 88, 177) but cannot vote.

- The six month rule covers the PM and CM too. Indira Gandhi (1966) and Manmohan Singh were Rajya Sabha members when appointed; Deve Gowda (1996) was a member of neither House and later entered the Rajya Sabha.

- Nominated Rajya Sabha members can be Ministers; nothing bars them.

- A Minister otherwise qualifies as a member does, with three extras: a 15% size cap on the Council of Ministers (Arts 75(1A), 164(1A); minimum 12 in States); a tribal welfare Minister in Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, MP and Odisha (Art 164(1)); and a disqualified defector cannot be a Minister until re-elected or the term ends (Arts 75(1B), 164(1B), 91st Amendment, 2003), nor hold a remunerative political post (Art 361B). Traps

- Nominated MPs vote for the Vice President but not the President.

- MLCs vote for neither.

- Delhi and Puducherry MLAs vote for the President; J&K MLAs count only once J&K has an elected Assembly. Special Intensive Revision (SIR)

- The ECI’s door to door reverification by Booth Level Officers (BLOs), followed by a draft roll and claims and objections; deletions are recorded under the Registration of Electors Rules, 1960. Legal basis: Art 324 and Sec 21(3), RPA 1950.

- Supreme Court (27 May 2026): A Bench of CJI Surya Kant and Justice Joymalya Bagchi upheld the SIR. The ECI may examine citizenship only for enrolment, not as a final declaration; Aadhaar is accepted as the 12th document, for identity, not citizenship. Traps

- The SIR revises the roll; it is not a citizenship determination. Overseas (NRI) electors (RP (Amendment) Act, 2010)

- A citizen living abroad who has not acquired foreign citizenship registers at the address in his passport (Form 6A, Sec 20A, RPA 1950).

- He must vote in person at his polling station in India. Traps

- No postal ballot and no proxy; the 2018 Bill for NRI proxy voting lapsed.

- OCI and PIO cardholders cannot enrol, as they are not citizens.
