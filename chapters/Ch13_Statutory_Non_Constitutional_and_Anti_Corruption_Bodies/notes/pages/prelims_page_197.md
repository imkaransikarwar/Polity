- **1.** Plan-making moves up - the NDMA and the SDMAs now prepare the national and State disaster management plans themselves, a function previously performed by the National Executive Committee and the State Executive Committees

- **2.** Statutory status for two pre-2005 bodies that had operated only by executive practice - the National Crisis Management Committee (NCMC), the nodal body for major crises, and the High Level Committee (HLC), which approves financial assistance out of the NDRF and the NDMF

- **3.** A State Government may constitute an Urban Disaster Management Authority (UDMA) for the State capital and every city having a Municipal Corporation, with Delhi and Chandigarh expressly excluded - Bengaluru was the first to be constituted

- **4.** States may raise a State Disaster Response Force (SDRF) of their own

- **5.** A national and State disaster database covering risk assessment, fund allocation, expenditure and preparedness

- **6.** The NDMA/SDMA also get the power to appoint experts and officers as they need

### DISASTER FUNDS VS THE DISASTER FORCE

- **1.** Funds - the National Disaster Response Fund (NDRF) and the State Disaster Response Fund (SDRF) meet relief and response costs (Disaster Management Act, 2005)

- **2.** The National and State Disaster Mitigation Funds (NDMF/SDMF) are exclusively for mitigation and cannot be diverted to relief (Disaster Management Act, 2005)

- **3.** The response funds are held in the Public Account, audited by the CAG, and releases from the NDRF are approved by the High Level Committee chaired by the Union Home Minister

- **4.** Following the 15th Finance Commission, the Centre contributes 75% of the SDRF for general States and 90% for the North Eastern and Himalayan States

- **5.** Force: the National Disaster Response Force (NDRF) is a specialist armed force of the Union, drawn from the CAPFs, headed by a Director General, working under the NDMA. It is a completely different entity from the fund with the same abbreviation (Disaster Management Act, 2005).

### STATE-LEVEL AND LOWER-TIER COUNTERPARTS

- **1.** The SDMA is chaired by the Chief Minister, with a State Executive Committee under the Chief Secretary (Disaster Management Act, 2005)

- **2.** The District Disaster Management Authority is chaired by the District Magistrate/Collector/Deputy Commissioner and co-chaired by the elected head of the district-level local authority - the Zila Parishad Chairperson where one exists; the co-chair is the detail usually missed (Disaster Management Act, 2005)
