| Parameter / Feature | National Human Rights Commission | State Human Rights Commission | Central Information Commission | State Information Commission |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Established [PRELIMS 2023] | Statutory body, 1993, under the Protection of Human Rights Act, 1993 | Statutory body, 1993, under the Protection of Human Rights Act, 1993 | Statutory body, 2005, under the Right to Information Act, 2005 | Statutory body, 2005, under the Right to Information Act, 2005 |
| Purpose | **1.** Watchdog of human rights<br>**2.** Human rights = rights relating to life, liberty, equality and dignity of the individual guaranteed by the Constitution or embodied in the International Covenants and enforceable by courts in India (Protection of Human Rights Act, 1993) | Can inquire into human rights violations only on subjects in the State List and the Concurrent List (Protection of Human Rights Act, 1993) | Entertains complaints and appeals pertaining to<br>**1.** Offices<br>**2.** Financial institutions<br>**3.** PSUs<br>**4.** Under the Central Government and UTs | Entertains complaints and appeals pertaining to<br>**1.** Offices<br>**2.** Financial institutions<br>**3.** PSUs<br>**4.** Under the State Government |
| Composition | Chairperson + 5 full time Members + 7 ex officio Members. The seven ex officio Members are:<br>**(a)** Chairpersons of the National Commissions for Minorities, SCs, STs, BCs, Women and Protection of Child Rights.<br>**(b)** The Chief Commissioner for Persons with Disabilities. | Chairperson + 2 Members | 1 Chief Information Commissioner + not more than 10 Information Commissioners (RTI Act, 2005) | 1 State Chief Information Commissioner + not more than 10 State Information Commissioners (RTI Act, 2005) |
| Head | Chairperson, who is or has been Chief Justice of India or a Judge of the Supreme Court | Chairperson, who is or has been Chief Justice or a Judge of a High Court | Chief Information Commissioner, a person of eminence in public life with wide knowledge and experience in law, science and technology, social service, management, journalism, mass media or administration and governance (RTI Act, 2005) | State Chief Information Commissioner, with the same eligibility as the CIC (RTI Act, 2005) |
| Member Qualification | **1.** 1 Member who is, or has been, a Judge of the Supreme Court<br>**2.** 1 Member who is, or has been, Chief Justice of a High Court<br>**3.** 3 Members, of whom at least 1 shall be a woman, having knowledge or practical experience in human rights | **1.** 1 Member who is, or has been, a Judge of a High Court or a District Judge in the State with a minimum of 7 years experience as District Judge<br>**2.** 1 Member having knowledge of or practical experience in matters relating to human rights | **1.** Persons of eminence in public life with wide knowledge and experience in law, science and technology, social service, management, journalism, mass media or administration and governance<br>**2.** No judicial background is required - in the Namit Sharma review (2013) the Supreme Court recalled its earlier 2012 direction | Same as CIC |
