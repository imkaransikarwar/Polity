## CHAPTER 05: STATES REORGANISATION

### THEME ANALYSIS PYQ pattern for this chapter

#### 1. REPEATS

#### A. The Four Fold Classification of 1950

- Part A Governor's Provinces, Part B Princely States under a Rajpramukh, Part C Chief Commissioner's Provinces, Part D the islands.

- Tripura as a Part 'C' State, then a centrally administered territory, then a full State [2025].

- The Seventh Constitutional Amendment Act, 1956 ended it, giving 14 States and 6 Union Territories.

#### B. Reorganisation Milestones

- Andhra State, 1953, the first linguistic State, capital Kurnool, after the fast by Potti Sriramulu [2008].

- The Dhar Commission, JVP Committee and Fazl Ali Commission behind the States Reorganisation Act, 1956.

- Later splits: Maharashtra and Gujarat, 1960; Punjab and Haryana, 1966; Telangana, 2014.

#### C. North Eastern States and their Legal Route

- Nagaland came through a Constitutional Amendment Act, not a reorganisation Act [2025].

- Meghalaya, Manipur and Tripura under the North Eastern Areas (Reorganisation) Act, 1971.

- Arunachal Pradesh and Mizoram as States in 1987; Itanagar named after the Ita fort [2025].

#### D. Article 3 and the Union Territories

- Article 3 lets Parliament alter boundaries by simple majority after referring the Bill to the State.

- Article 4 places such laws outside Article 368, so they are not amendments.

- Sikkim by the Thirty Sixth Amendment, 1975; Jammu and Kashmir and Ladakh as Union Territories in 2019.

#### 2. HOW THE FORMAT HAS CHANGED

- 2008: one line recall of a capital [2008]

- 2025: a three pair set counted with "how many pairs" [2025]

#### 3. WHAT IS BEING ASKED NOW

- Composite items fusing polity with geography: capitals, national parks and the legal route to statehood [2025]

- The instrument behind each State (Act or Constitutional Amendment) matters more than the year
