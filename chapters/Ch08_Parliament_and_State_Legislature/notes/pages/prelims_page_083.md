| ATTRIBUTE | Lok Sabha | State Legislative Assembly | Rajya Sabha | State Legislative Council |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Constitutional basis | Article 79 to 122 | Article 168 to 212 | Same as LS | Same as SLA |
| Organisation | Parliament = President + Rajya Sabha + Lok Sabha | **1.** State Legislature = Governor + SLA, and an SLC where one exists.<br>**2.** No uniformity; most States are unicameral | Part of Parliament | Optional House, created or abolished under Art 169 |
| Composition [PRELIMS 2015] | **1.** Max 550 (Art 81): 530 States + 20 UTs.<br>**2.** Anglo Indian nomination (Art 331) ceased 25 Jan 2020 under the 104th Amendment, 2019, so the old ceiling of 552 is now 550.<br>**3.** Present strength 543 | Max 500, min 60 (Art 170) | **1.** Max 250 (Art 80): 238 representatives of States and UTs + 12 nominated by the President.<br>**2.** Present strength 245 (233 + 12) | Max one third of the Assembly strength, min 40 (Art 171) |
| Election | Territorial constituencies, direct election, first past the post | Same as LS | **1.** Proportional representation by single transferable vote, by the elected members of the SLA (Art 80(4)).<br>**2.** 12 nominated by the President for art, literature, science and social service. | - 1/3 elected by local bodies (municipalities, district boards etc.) -1/12 elected by graduates of 3 years’ standing residing in the state -1/12 elected by teachers with 3 years’ experience in institutions not below secondary level -1/3 elected by MLAs from among non-MLAs Remainder (~1/6) nominated by the Governor Method: Local body, graduate and teacher members chosen from territorial constituencies; all elections by proportional representation through single transferable vote.. |
| Duration | **1.** 5 years from its first meeting, then automatic dissolution.<br>**2.** The President may dissolve it at any time. | Same scheme, the Governor dissolving, with the proviso to Art 172(1) | **1.** Permanent chamber, not subject to dissolution. | Same as the Rajya Sabha |
