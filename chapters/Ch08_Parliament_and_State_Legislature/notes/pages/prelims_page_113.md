| DEVICE | RULE AND BASIS | FUNCTION | HOW IT DIFFERS FROM THE SIX ABOVE |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Question Hour and Supplementary Questions | Rules 32 to 54, Lok Sabha. The first hour of every sitting. | Starred questions receive an oral answer and admit supplementaries; unstarred questions receive a written answer; short notice questions concern matters of urgent public importance at less than ten days' notice. | **1.** An information-extracting device rather than a censure device. |
| Half-an-Hour Discussion | Rule 55, Lok Sabha. Rule 60, Rajya Sabha. | Discussion on a matter of sufficient public importance that has already been the subject of a question, where the answer needs elucidation on a point of fact. | **1.** Anchored to a prior question, unlike a Short Duration Discussion.<br>**2.** No formal motion and no vote. |
| Special Mention (Rajya Sabha) and Rule 377 (Lok Sabha) | Rules 180A to 180E, Rajya Sabha, in force from 1 July 2000. Rule 377, Lok Sabha. | Allow a member to bring a matter that is not a point of order and cannot be raised under any other rule to the notice of the House. | **1.** Purely a device for placing a matter on record.<br>**2.** No discussion, no ministerial reply as of right, and no vote. |
| Motion of Thanks | Rule 16 onwards, Lok Sabha. Moved on the President's Address under Article 87. | The House thanks the President for the Address; amendments may be moved and are put to vote. | **1.** The only one of these devices grounded directly in the Constitution.<br>**2.** Its defeat amounts to a defeat of the government, which is why it is treated as a confidence test. |
