### Way Forward

- **1.** Mandatory Referral: Amend Rules of Procedure to make bill referral to committees the "default" rather than "discretionary." (UK System)

- **2.** Extended Tenures: Increase the term of committee members from 1 year to 2 or 5 years for expertise.

- **3.** Binding Nature (Selective): Make "unanimous" recommendations binding on the government.

- **4.** Dedicated Research Staff: Every DRSC should have a team of subject-matter experts and legal researchers similar to U.S. Congressional Research Service (CRS)

- **5.** Transparency: Live-stream committee hearings (excluding sensitive security matters) to ensure public accountability.

- **6.** Inter-Session Meetings: Encourage committees to meet more frequently during the "Inter-Session" period to reduce pendency.

- **7.** Gender Parity: Ensure that every committee has at least 33% women members, following the spirit of the 2026 Delimitation Bill.

- **8.** Mandatory Response from Government: Ministries should be legally required to respond within a fixed timeline (Eg. 3 months) to DRSC recommendations.

### Ethics committee

The Ethics Committee serves as the "moral guardian" of the Indian Parliament, ensuring that the conduct of legislators aligns with the dignity of the House.

### History and Origin

- **1.** 1996 Presiding Officers' Conference in New Delhi recommended ethical oversight bodies.

- **2.** Rajya Sabha: The first such committee in any Indian legislature, constituted in 1997.

- **3.** LS: An ad hoc committee was formed in 2000. Became permanent committee in 2015. LS committee has the strength of 15 members and RS has 10 members.

### Key Powers and Functions

- **1.** Overseeing Moral Conduct: Monitors the behavior of members to ensure it meets the ethical standards expected of people's representatives.

- **2.** Examination of Complaints: Investigates specific cases of "ethical misconduct" referred by the Presiding Officer or other members.
