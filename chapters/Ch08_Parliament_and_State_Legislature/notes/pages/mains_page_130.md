Constitution (106th Amendment) Act, 2023 The Constitution (106th Amendment) Act, 2023,reserves one-third of all seats for women in Lok Sabha, State legislative assemblies, and the Legislative Assembly of the National Capital Territory of Delhi,including those reserved for SCs and STs. Key Provisions:

- Articles Amended: Article 239AA: Reserves one-third of the seats for women in the Legislative Assembly of the NCT of Delhi.

- Articles Inserted: ◦ Article 330A: Reserves one-third of the seats for women in the Lok Sabha, including seats reserved for SCs/STs under Article 330. ◦ Article 332A: Reserves one-third of the seats for women in State Legislative Assemblies, including seats reserved for SCs/STs under Article 332. ◦ Article 334A: Provisions take effect after delimitation following the first census post-act commencement. ◦ Sunset Clause: Reservation ceases 15 years after implementation but can be extended by Parliament. ◦ Periodic rotation of reserved seats after each delimitation, as determined by Parliament. ◦ No impact on current legislative representation until dissolution.
