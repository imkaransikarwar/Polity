- **5.** Sixth Schedule autonomous district councils: he audits them and reports to the Governor. What he does not do

- **1.** Panchayats and municipalities: there is no statutory CAG audit. Since the 11th Finance Commission, he provides only Technical Guidance and Supervision (TGS) over the State’s Local Fund Audit. Individual local bodies can still be entrusted to him under the CAG (DPC) Act, 1971.

- **2.** Secret service expenditure: he cannot call for the details. He accepts a certificate from the competent executive authority that the money was spent under its authority. 3. “Comptroller” is a misnomer: unlike the British CAG, he has no control over the issue of money from the Consolidated Fund. The executive draws money without his sanction, so in substance he is only an Auditor General.
